Welcome to Falling Platforms Survival! While you focus on jumping between platforms and staying alive as long as possible, we focus on keeping your privacy safe.

1. No Data Collection
Falling Platforms Survival does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected
No gameplay activity is tracked outside your device
No IP addresses, device identifiers, or usage logs are stored

2. No Ads or Tracking Systems
The game is designed to provide a clean and focused survival experience.

No advertisements are displayed
No analytics or tracking tools are used
No third-party services collect user data

3. Safe for All Ages
Because no data is collected, Falling Platforms Survival is safe for players of all ages, including children under 13.

4. No Sensitive Permissions Required
The game works without requesting access to private device features.

No Camera or Microphone access
No Contacts or Messages access
No Location Services

5. Local Data Storage Only
All game-related data is saved directly on your device.

Progress and high scores are stored locally
No external servers or cloud storage
Removing the app deletes stored data

6. Policy Updates
If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the core promise of protecting your privacy will remain unchanged.
